Legal guides

Common legal questions, explained clearly.

A practical starting point for common concerns in the Philippines. These guides are for general information only; the facts and documents of each situation matter.

01

Family protection and RA 9262

RA 9262, or the Anti-Violence Against Women and Their Children Act, addresses physical, sexual, psychological, and economic abuse in covered relationships.

Prepare: A timeline, messages, medical or police records, and information about immediate safety concerns.

02

Contracts and obligations

The Civil Code provides the basic framework for agreements, consent, obligations, breach, damages, and remedies. Clear terms and complete records are important before signing.

Prepare: The complete contract, amendments, receipts, messages, and the specific promise or deadline in question.

03

Property and land concerns

Ownership, boundaries, leases, deeds, titles, tax declarations, and sales often involve several documents and overlapping facts that should be reviewed together.

Prepare: Copies of the title, tax declaration, deed or lease, survey documents, receipts, and a short history of the property.

04

Succession and estates

Inheritance may involve a will, compulsory heirs, estate settlement, partition, property records, and taxes. The family relationship and ownership history can change the process.

Prepare: Death certificate, birth or marriage records, will if available, asset list, titles, and known debts.

05

Labor and employment

Employment concerns may involve wages, benefits, leave, disciplinary action, termination, workplace policies, or independent-contractor arrangements under the Labor Code and related rules.

Prepare: Contract, payslips, notices, company policies, attendance records, and a dated account of what happened.

06

Criminal process and due process

A complaint, investigation, subpoena, arrest, or court case has different stages and deadlines. A person involved should understand the document received and avoid ignoring formal notices.

Prepare: The complaint or notice, dates, identification, supporting records, witness details, and any prior communication from authorities.

A careful note

This page provides general educational information and is not legal advice. Laws, procedures, and deadlines may change, and the right next step depends on the specific facts. A consultation is recommended before signing documents, making admissions, or allowing a deadline to pass.
