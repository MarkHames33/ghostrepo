Frequently asked questions

A clearer start to your legal concern.

These answers explain how to prepare and what to expect. The right advice depends on the facts and documents of each matter.

What should I bring to an initial consultation?

Bring a valid ID and the documents connected to your concern, such as contracts, notices, receipts, titles, messages, or court papers. A short timeline of important events is also useful.

Can I ask about fees before meeting?

Yes. Fees and scope depend on the nature, complexity, urgency, and service requested. The office can discuss the expected next step and applicable fees after learning more about the matter.

Do you provide notarial services?

Yes. Notarial work may include affidavits, acknowledgments, contracts, authorizations, and other documents requiring notarization. Bring the complete document and the required identification.

Does sending a message create an attorney-client relationship?

No. Online messages are for initial coordination only. An attorney-client relationship is created only when the office formally accepts the engagement.

What if my concern is urgent?

Call the office and explain the deadline or immediate risk. If there is an immediate danger to life or safety, contact the appropriate emergency authorities first.

What types of legal matters may the office assist with?

The office may assist with consultations, contracts and agreements, document review, notarial services, property concerns, family matters, business questions, negotiation, and formal representation when appropriate.

Can I send documents before my consultation?

You may send a short summary and relevant document details through the office's available contact channels. Do not send highly sensitive information until the office confirms the appropriate way to share it.

Are online consultations available?

Online coordination may be possible depending on the matter, documents, schedule, and technology available. Contact the office to confirm whether an online consultation is suitable.

What identification is needed for notarization?

Bring an acceptable government-issued identification card and the complete document. The names and details must be checked carefully, and witnesses or additional records may be required depending on the document.

Is my information kept confidential?

Information is handled with discretion and used for initial coordination and legal assistance as appropriate. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive details through public or unsecured channels.
